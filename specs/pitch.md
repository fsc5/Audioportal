# Audioportal Pitch

## Usecase

I often use my laptop to watch videos while playing games on my pc.
To have audio shared between the two I use Voicemeeter.

The program is fine, but its hard to configure and shows me alot of options i dont need.

I want a app that just gives you a local audio device and simple configuration out of the box.

For the first version you still need to manually add the ip of the other device, but in future i want to add auto discover.

## Technology

The appp starts webapp on localhost through which everything can be configured.
Through it you can configure the inputs and outputs.

Elixir with Liveview might be a good pick, but lets discuss on this.
